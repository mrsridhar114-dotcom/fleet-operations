/Administration/Storage & backup

Storage & backup

How much of the app's storage is used, and a backup of every record.

Attached files

Photos and PDFs: RC, insurance, licences, ID proofs, slips

Records

Every saved entry, by area

Backup

Keep these somewhere safe, such as Google Drive or email, and take them at least once a month. Attached photos and PDFs stay in the app and are not inside the downloads.

Trip sheets: one sheet per truck in your truck income / expense format (round trips with trip balance and payment status), and one per route bus with every day's trip sheet. Bookings: one sheet per spare bus in your spare bus income / expense format, plus the full booking register and all payments. Totals and balances are live Excel formulas. Full backup holds every record, including vehicles, drivers, conductors, users and change history, and is the file to keep for restoring.
